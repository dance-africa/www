Privacy & your data

Request preparation

The request form prepares a JSON file on your device. It does not transmit your name, email address or request to Dance Africa. Downloaded files may contain the personal details you entered: share them only with a contact you trust, and delete them when no longer needed.

Workspace preview

Editorial drafts are stored in your browser’s local storage. They are not private from other people using the same browser profile. Do not enter member data, credentials or confidential information. You can delete individual drafts in the workspace or clear this site’s data in your browser settings. Export any drafts you want to keep first.

External services

Following the Facebook link takes you to a separate service with its own privacy terms. This site does not load Facebook tracking scripts, advertising pixels or third-party fonts.

Hosting and future registration

The hosting provider may process connection information when serving the site. Member registration, analytics, email marketing and social publishing are not connected in this release. A complete registration privacy notice, a named data controller and a contact channel must be provided before those services launch.
